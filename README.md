## Project Objective

The objective of this task was to create an interactive Power BI dashboard
for analyzing sales performance and customer behavior.

The dashboard provides insights into total sales, orders, customers,
categories, cities, age groups, and monthly sales trends.


## Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Excel
- DAX

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales: 139.40M
- Total Orders: 1K
- Total Customers: 947
- Average Order Value: 139.40K


## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Total Customers KPI
- Average Order Value KPI
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 5 Cities by Sales
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Age Group
- Interactive slicers for filtering the data
- Deep Dive Analysis page

Business Insights

1.Overall Sales Performance
Total sales reached approximately 139.40M, indicating strong overall sales activity during the analyzed period.
2.Customer Base
The dashboard contains 947 customers, providing a useful view of customer purchasing behavior.
3.Category Performance
Electronics is among the stronger-performing categories and contributes significantly to overall sales.
4.City Performance
Patna appears among the leading cities in the Top 5 Cities by Sales analysis.
5.Age Group Analysis
Sales are distributed across multiple age groups, from 18–25 through 56–65, allowing the business to understand which customer segments contribute to sales.
6.Monthly Sales Trend
Sales change across different months, helping identify periods of relatively higher and lower sales activity.


## Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive way to understand sales
performance and customer behavior. It helps identify important sales
categories, high-performing cities, customer age groups, and monthly
sales patterns.

The dashboard can be used to support data-driven business decisions
and identify areas for further analysis.
